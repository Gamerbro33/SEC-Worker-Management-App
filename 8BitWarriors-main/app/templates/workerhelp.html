Help & Instructions

Welcome to the Worker Dashboard! Here's a quick guide to navigating the application:

  • Home: This page displays a map showing your current location and the location of your assigned jobsite. You can use this to visualize your proximity to the site.
  • Jobsites: Here you can find detailed information about the jobsites you are currently assigned to. This may include the address, site contact, and specific instructions.
  • Profile: Access your profile by clicking the icon in the top right corner. On this page, you can update your profile picture and change your password.
  • Directions: On the Jobsite details page, you'll find a "Get Directions" button. Clicking this will open your device's default map application with the route to the jobsite pre-filled.
  • Sidebar Navigation: Use the menu icon (☰) to open and close the sidebar for navigating between the Home, Jobsites, and Help pages. The sidebar also contains the Logout button.
  • Logout: To securely log out of the application, open the sidebar and click the "Logout" button. You will be asked to confirm before logging out.

If you encounter any issues or have questions not covered here, please reach out to your Project Manager for assistance.
